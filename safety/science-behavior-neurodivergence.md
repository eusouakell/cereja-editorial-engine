# Science, behavior and neurodivergence policy

Separate:
- lived experience;
- workplace observation;
- scientific evidence;
- clinical guidance;
- opinion/hypothesis.

Prefer primary research, systematic reviews/meta-analyses and recognized scientific/public-health institutions.

Avoid:
- diagnosis by content;
- universal claims about neurodivergent people;
- causal language from correlational research;
- treatment recommendations;
- overstating single studies;
- using client work as proof without permission/evidence.

Editorial bridges may include communication, cognitive load, remote work design, learning, sensory/environmental conditions, documentation and collaboration.
