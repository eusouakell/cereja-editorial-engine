# Token taxonomy

Status: **working taxonomy**. Expand only from observed editorial use.

| Type | Function | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| `signal` | Something newly observed or changing | research, news, market scan |
| `evidence` | Factual support for a claim | primary/strong source |
| `thesis` | Kell/Cereja interpretation or position | approved thesis record |
| `counterpoint` | Material tension, limitation or alternative reading | research / Devil's Advocate |
| `question` | Opens or advances inquiry | editorial decision |
| `cultural-reference` | Connects a subject to culture/media/design | editorial curation |
| `example` | Makes an abstract idea concrete | source or experience |
| `metaphor` | Optional explanatory device | authored editorial choice |
| `recommendation` | Tool, book, paper, company or object worth following | curation |
| `cta` | Invitation to read, answer, save, subscribe or explore | channel template |
| `transition` | Connective tissue between blocks | authored editorial choice |

## Composition levels

The taxonomy describes **meaning**. Composition levels describe **structure**.

- **Token** — traceable semantic unit.
- **Atom** — minimum publishable expression: hook, question, fact card, quote card.
- **Molecule** — short combination: mini-argument, three-card sequence, short section.
- **Organism** — complete editorial structure: AiaiAI block, metapost, visual carousel.
- **Template** — reusable grammar with required inputs and constraints.
- **Publication** — final reviewed instance for a specific channel.

Do not force every publication through every level. The model exists to make reuse inspectable, not bureaucratic.
