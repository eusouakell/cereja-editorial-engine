# Multiformat channel-fit evaluation

Status: proposed review criteria.

Channel-fit evaluation sits **after factual/evidence checks**. A well-adapted format cannot rescue an unsupported claim.

## Shared checks

- Does the output preserve the Editorial Packet thesis?
- Did adaptation introduce a new factual claim?
- Is claim scope unchanged?
- Is the piece useful without requiring knowledge of another channel?
- Does it sound specific to Cereja/Kell rather than generic AI copy?
- Are rights and attribution requirements preserved?
- Is the call to action appropriate to the channel?
- Is Kell's approval still required? **Yes.**

## LinkedIn

Check:
- self-contained argument;
- useful professional implication where relevant;
- not merely promotion for the newsletter;
- no manufactured certainty or contrarianism.

## Instagram

Check:
- visual sequence has a reason to be visual;
- card density and pacing vary;
- cultural/reference material is legible and attributable;
- no listicle pattern forced onto narrative material.

## Stories

Check:
- provisional status is clear when needed;
- source can be recovered;
- ephemeral format is not used to bypass evidence or rights review.
