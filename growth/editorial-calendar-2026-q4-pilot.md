      ↓

      ↓
EDITORIAL PACKET
      ↓

      ↓

|---|---|---|---|

| QA | Voice / Channel-fit / Rights / Accessibility | Source Checker | pass/block evidence |
| publish | Kell | — | human decision |
| learn-back | Archivist | Growth / Workflow | outcomes + effort + decisions |

Local role names do not activate new canonical Factory agents.

## Human-attention instrumentation

For each cycle record:
- planned human gates;
- unplanned Kell interventions;
- reason for each intervention;
- convergence rounds;
- repeated blocker class;
- what Kell rewrote or removed;
- what was cancelled because channel fit/effort was weak.

The goal is not zero human involvement. It is to remove human traffic management while preserving consequential editorial authority.

## Pilot end decision

For each of Medium, DEV.to, YouTube and Story Craft:

`KEEP / ADJUST / STOP / NEED MORE EVIDENCE`

Record:
- job served;
- outcome signals;
- human effort;
- distinct value vs other channels;
- recommendation for 2027.
