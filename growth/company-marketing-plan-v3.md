## Marketing system

```text
SIGNALS / REAL WORK
        ↓
RESEARCH + KNOWLEDGE
        ↓
EDITORIAL PACKET
        ↓
NATIVE PUBLICATIONS + PUBLIC PROOF
        ↓
DISCOVERY + CONVERSATION
        ↓
AUTHORITY + OWNED AUDIENCE
        ↓
OPPORTUNITIES + PRODUCT/IP SIGNALS
        ↓
LEARN-BACK
        ↺
```

Two loops remain coupled but distinct:

### Authority / audience
curate → interpret → publish → converse/be discovered → accumulate evidence → recognition/opportunity → better signals.

### IP / product
build/solve → test → document → teach → package → use/sell/implement → new evidence.

**Lab bridges the loops.**

## 2026 Q4 pilot OKRs

These OKRs govern the 8-week pilot. They prioritize learning and qualified signals over vanity volume because current baselines are incomplete.

### O1 — Make Context Engineering and AI-enabled knowledge work legible through credible public proof

**KR1.** Complete four newsletter-led editorial cycles with at least four durable proof/depth artifacts across GitHub, DEV.to, Medium or YouTube; each artifact must preserve provenance and have a clear job.

**KR2.** Generate at least **3 qualified authority signals** during the pilot — speaking invitation, media/podcast invitation, collaboration request, substantive professional/commercial inbound, citation/reference or equivalent. If the result is zero, record zero; do not substitute impressions.

**KR3.** By pilot review, identify at least **2 reusable proof artifacts** strong enough to support Kell's portfolio/speaker evidence inventory.

> The threshold in KR2 is a pilot hypothesis, not a historical benchmark.

### O2 — Grow owned audience and relationship depth with measurable attribution limits

**KR1.** Before the #32 launch, capture the available baseline for Substack, Instagram, LinkedIn, site analytics and referral/source visibility, explicitly marking unavailable metrics.

**KR2.** End the pilot with **positive net newsletter subscriber growth** versus the baseline while tracking cancellations separately.

**KR3.** By the pilot review, identify the **top two discovery paths** and **top two depth signals** actually observed (for example replies, substantive comments, sends/shares, saves, return/read behavior, qualified click/conversation), without comparing incompatible raw metrics.

### O3 — Prove that the editorial system can compound without consuming disproportionate human attention

**KR1.** 100% of newsletter cycles have a recoverable Editorial Packet, applicable narrative/discoverability review, human publication gate and learn-back.

**KR2.** Record planned vs. unplanned Kell interventions and convergence/rework rounds in every cycle; routine specialist disagreement must not escalate before the declared internal resolution path is exhausted.

**KR3.** Show a downward or stable-bounded trend in avoidable rework from the first half of the pilot to the second; if rework rises, identify the earliest owning stage causing it.

**KR4.** On 2026-12-07 classify Medium, DEV.to, YouTube and Story Craft as **KEEP / ADJUST / STOP / NEED MORE EVIDENCE** using both outcome signal and human effort.

## Reuse rule

Do not cascade copy:

```text
newsletter → shorter LinkedIn → shorter Instagram
```

Compose from the packet:

```text
EDITORIAL PACKET
      ↓
CONTENT TOKENS
  ↙    ↓    ↘
native channel compositions
```

A channel may select different tokens, structure and emphasis. It may not invent evidence, widen claim scope, create Kell's opinion or publish without authority.

## Discoverability

At packet/channel-selection time, consider:
- likely search question;
- durable entities/terms;
- evergreen vs timely life;
- public proof/repository worth linking;
- canonical destination when applicable;
- metadata/title language.

Search demand may shape packaging. It does not create facts or thesis.

## One-person sustainability

Before adding recurring work:
1. distinct job?
2. evidence of audience or strategic need?
3. fed by existing research without mechanical duplication?
4. approval/production burden acceptable?
5. what is reduced or stopped if this begins?

YouTube is a **capacity ceiling**, not a quota. Medium and DEV.to stay pilots until the December review.

## Measurement

### Authority
- qualified inbound;
- speaking/media invitations;
- collaborations;
- citations/references;
- proof reused in portfolio/speaker context.

### Owned audience / depth
- net subscribers;
- replies/conversations;
- returning/deep reading where available;
- sends/shares/saves using rates where appropriate;
- referral/source visibility;
- direct/branded discovery when measurable.

### Production health
- planned/unplanned human interventions;
- rework rounds;
- blockers by stage;
- effort/time when intentionally measured;
- pieces cancelled because channel fit was weak;
- keep/adjust/stop decisions.

Do not treat impressions/follower count as primary success measures.
