## What the cell confirmed

1. Newsletter should remain quinzenal and central to editorial depth.
2. Editorial Packet should remain the shared working object.
3. Native composition by channel is stronger than newsletter-first repurposing.
4. Instagram should remain a visual magazine rather than a generic marketing-carousel operation.
5. LinkedIn is an authority surface, not a newsletter notification channel.
6. Medium, DEV.to and YouTube have sufficiently distinct jobs to justify a pilot.
7. GitHub should remain proof/methods, not the default reader funnel.
8. One-person sustainability must constrain channel growth.

## Adjustments required before approval

### A. Add Narrative Craft & Oral Storytelling

The current system is strong on thesis, evidence, channel fit and QA but can still produce correct material that reads as exposition rather than an experience.

Add a cross-format craft layer for:
- tension / stakes;
- action + reflection;
- expectation / result gap;
- scene, object and concrete detail;
- oral rhythm and presence;
- situated voice;
- observation / listening;
- cultural montage;
- timing / surprise.

Story is not compulsory. The first decision is whether the material benefits from a story route.

### B. Make discoverability explicit earlier

Medium, DEV.to, YouTube and evergreen web artifacts can compound authority beyond feed distribution.

At packet/channel-selection time record:
- likely search question;
- durable terms/entities;
- public proof to link;
- canonical destination if one exists;
- whether the piece is timely, evergreen or both.

Do not let search demand create a thesis or inflate claim scope.

### C. Protect human attention

The main operational risk is not lack of ideas; it is review burden.

Record:
- planned Kell gates;
- unplanned interventions;
- rework rounds;
- publish/keep/stop decision per pilot channel.

Routine specialist disagreement should resolve internally before escalation.

### D. Treat video as a capacity ceiling, not a quota

YouTube is strategically valuable because it can create:
- spoken explanation;
- demonstration;
- speaking evidence;
- reusable media;
- discovery.

Start with one video. Continue monthly only if the first run's effort and quality justify it.

### E. Add explicit OKRs

Channel cadence must be subordinate to outcomes:
- legible authority/proof;
- owned audience + depth;
- sustainable operation.

Avoid follower/reach targets without baseline.

## Opportunities the earlier plan underweighted

1. **Evergreen discoverability** — long-form and video can continue working after the social cycle ends.
2. **Speaking proof** — strong video can demonstrate teaching/presence to event organizers, not merely generate views.
3. **Knowledge lineage** — every public artifact should be traceable back to packet/evidence and forward to proof/learn-back.
4. **Search + AI citation surfaces** — inspectable, sourced public artifacts can strengthen discoverability independently of social feeds.
5. **Narrative differentiation** — Brazilian oral/reportage traditions can prevent the Cereja voice from becoming an English-language creator framework translated into Portuguese.
