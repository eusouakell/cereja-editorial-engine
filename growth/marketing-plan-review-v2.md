## Summary decision

**KEEP the current distribution plan as a property-level operating plan.  
REVISE the company-level marketing strategy before expanding acquisition or paid media.**

The current plan is careful and operationally mature, but it optimizes a narrower problem: acquire and retain readers for Cereja Flamejante.

The current business goal is broader:

- make Kell's authority visible and portable;
- build association with Context Engineering while preserving the wider Knowledge / Learning / Innovation repertoire;
- create speaking and qualified professional inbound;
- grow owned audience;
- turn Lab work into public proof and reusable IP;
- create paths into Academy, Studio and Products;
- preserve Cereja Flamejante as an author-led editorial property instead of B2B-izing it.

Keep this.

Keep this.

Keep this.

Keep this.

## Strategic gaps and opportunities

| Finding | Why it matters | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Goal is subscriber growth, not company growth | Newsletter acquisition cannot measure authority, speaking, inbound, IP or commercial learning | Add company objectives above property metrics |
| Audience model is too reader-centric | Current hypotheses omit C-level, practitioners/builders, buyers, event organizers and professional inbound | Use a multi-audience model with progressive disclosure |
| LinkedIn is treated mainly as post-edition distribution | Kell already has a larger personal network and needs public proof of expertise | Make personal LinkedIn a primary authority surface for build-in-public and applied thinking |
| Website is framed as newsletter calling card | Cereja is evolving into a company with Media, Lab, Academy, Studio / Products | Keep current site functional now; plan a later company IA redesign |
| Personal portfolio is outside the funnel | Authority must remain portable across employers and Cereja | Add `eusouakell.com.br` as a separate authority/conversion surface |
| No explicit speaking funnel | Speaking is a target outcome, not an accidental benefit | Track speaking-ready evidence, organizer discovery and invitations |
| No IP/product capture loop | Good editorial/system work can disappear after publication | Capture reusable frameworks, evals, templates and tools as Product candidates |
| One linear funnel | Audience, authority, product learning and revenue have different feedback cycles | Use two coupled flywheels joined by Lab evidence |
| Multimodal is under-specified | Kell's capability and intended offers include audio, video, facilitation, events and tools | Model modality by job, not only by social format |
| Paid-media study appears too early in sequence | There is no stable company proposition, baseline or end-to-end instrumentation yet | Keep as research only; require instrumentation + organic evidence before spend |
| Discoverability is not yet a system | Search, AI citation and public evidence can compound authority independently of social feeds | Add SEO / Search Console / structured authority work later |
| Second editorial property is unresolved | A business/technical publication could support demand, but launching now adds operational load | Test its editorial job first through personal LinkedIn / articles |
| Old consulting/learning method is disconnected from new AI work | This can make positioning look narrower than actual capability | Integrate Knowledge + Learning + Context + Innovation at company level |
| Career optionality is absent | Professional inbound is a strategic outcome for Kell | Add qualified career/professional inbound to authority metrics |

## Flywheel evaluation

The two candidate logics should **not** be collapsed into one.

### Loop A — authority and audience

```text
signals
→ curation
→ interpretation
→ native publication
→ conversation/discovery
→ public evidence + recognition
→ better sources/network/opportunities
→ signals
```

Use for:
- Cereja Flamejante;
- personal LinkedIn;
- speaking;
- discovery;
- reputation.

### Loop B — IP, product and revenue

```text
real problem / Lab build
→ test + evaluate
→ document
→ teach / publish
→ productize
→ use / sell / implement
→ new evidence
→ better method
```

Use for:
- Lab;
- Academy;
- Studio;
- Products.

### Why both

A single content-to-lead funnel would over-commercialize the media property.

A single build-to-product loop would underinvest in audience, interpretation and cultural authority.

**Lab is the bridge.**

## Audience architecture

### Executive / C-level

Needs:
- consequence;
- decision;
- risk;
- organizational implication;
- adoption/governance;
- concise evidence.

### Builders / practitioners

Needs:
- architecture;
- implementation;
- agents, skills, evals and workflows;
- failures and trade-offs;
- repositories, tools and examples.

### Conversion audiences

Not editorial personas, but important paths:
- event organizers / media;
- potential buyers;
- collaborators;
- recruiters / organizations seeking senior expertise.

One topic may have multiple depths without forcing all material into one post.

## Authority metrics

Do not use follower count as the primary measure.

Track categories such as:
- qualified newsletter growth;
- returning/deep readers where measurable;
- GitHub visits/stars/forks only when meaningful to the project;
- direct replies and substantive conversations;
- references/citations/mentions;
- speaking invitations;
- podcast/interview/event invitations;
- collaboration requests;
- qualified professional inbound;
- Product/Academy interest;
- Studio conversations;
- client conversion when a commercial offer exists.

## Portfolio dependency

The personal portfolio requires a separate substantial workstream.

Before implementation:
- strategy and evidence model should stabilize;
- public evidence inventory should be assembled;
- speaking architecture should be defined;
- relation between Kell and Cereja should be explicit.

Then connect Codex to the appropriate portfolio/site source through MCP or another approved integration and execute with bounded repository/site authority.

Do not redesign the portfolio in parallel with every current Cereja foundation task.

## Paid media gate

Keep the existing paid-media study as research.

Do not recommend spend until all of the following are true:

1. GA4 / measurement is working for the relevant destination.
2. Organic message/content has produced a usable baseline.
3. The destination has one clear job.
4. Audience and promise are explicit.
5. Rights and consent requirements are satisfied.
6. The team can respond to the traffic/lead outcome.
7. Kell sets a spend and stopping rule.

Paid media should amplify evidence, not compensate for unclear positioning.
