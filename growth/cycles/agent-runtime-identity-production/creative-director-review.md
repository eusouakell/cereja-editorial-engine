## Learn-back

Relevant conceptual copy should not be left as microcopy if it matters to comprehension. If it cannot be comfortably read at channel scale, either promote it in hierarchy or remove it from the visual and carry it in caption/alt text.

Illustrative credential microcopy is different: it may remain small because its primary job is to signal system identity and materiality, not carry the post's core argument.
