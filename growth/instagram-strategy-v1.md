# Instagram Strategy V1 — Cereja Flamejante

Status: **working channel strategy — human-approved publication remains required**.
Date: 2026-10-05.

This strategy sits under:

- [Company Marketing Plan V2](company-marketing-plan-v2.md)
- [Distribution Plan](distribution-plan.md)

It defines Instagram's editorial job, content architecture, experiment model and quality gates without turning Cereja Flamejante into an "algorithm content" account.

## 1. Channel job

Instagram is the **visual discovery layer** of Cereja Flamejante.

Its job is to make a person stop, recognize an interesting object or connection, and want to:

- send it to someone;
- save it;
- continue the thought;
- visit the profile;
- read/subscribe when the edition is the natural next step.

It is **not**:

- a compressed copy of the newsletter;
- a quote-card factory;
- a daily posting obligation;
- a generic "AI + tech" content channel;
- the canonical knowledge system;
- a place where every topic must become a carousel.

## 2. Working platform model

Treat these as the current working distribution signals:

- **watch time / retention**;
- **likes per reach**;
- **sends/shares per reach**, especially for reaching people who do not already follow the account;
- **originality / recommendation eligibility**.

Do not convert this into hard numeric targets until Cereja has its own baseline.

Do not assume every third-party "viral benchmark" is causal or transferable.

### Source hierarchy

1. Meta / Instagram product and recommendation documentation;
2. public statements from Instagram leadership;
3. account-level evidence from Cereja;
4. external creator/agency benchmarks;
5. heuristics.

## 3. BrandsDecoded benchmark — use and boundary

BrandsDecoded is useful as a **competitive/editorial benchmark**, not as a canonical recipe.

Useful patterns to study:

- trend analysis with a journalistic angle;
- explanatory carousels that teach one mechanism;
- fast single-slide news/opinion when the object is genuinely timely;
- headline testing;
- systematic review of outliers rather than relying only on intuition;
- explicit anti-generic-content filtering;
- channel-native visual design.

Do **not** copy:

- claims that a template is "viral" by default;
- fixed nine-slide choreography for every subject;
- algorithm-bait headlines that weaken Cereja's editorial credibility;
- visual treatment that makes every post look like the same content machine;
- sales claims such as "5–10x chance of viralization" as evidence.

The benchmark becomes a **hypothesis generator**.

Cereja's advantage is not that it can produce the same high-performing carousel faster.

It is that Kell's curation can make connections and select objects that another account would not choose in the same way.

## 4. Content architecture

Instagram uses **content jobs**, not mandatory columns.

### A. Unexpected connection

Job: connect two objects that create recognition or surprise and preserve the real path of discovery.

Best formats: visual carousel; Reel when motion/audio materially improves the connection.

Primary signal hypothesis: sends.

### B. Trend / cultural analysis

Job: take one current phenomenon and explain the interesting mechanism beneath it; avoid generic trend-report language.

Best format: 4–8 frame carousel; single-slide signal when the fact itself carries the post.

Primary signal hypothesis: sends + follows/profile visits.

### C. Useful explainer

Job: teach one mechanism, tool, pattern or "how this works"; give the reader something concrete to use or understand.

Best format: carousel; screen-recorded Reel when interaction matters.

Primary signal hypothesis: saves + sends.

### D. Visual essay / reference desk

Job: make an object worth looking at; foreground photography, archive, design, film, books, interfaces or artifacts with provenance.

Best format: carousel / VisualMagazine.

Primary signal hypothesis: saves + sends.

### E. Signal / one-slide post

Job: react quickly to one consequential or delightful object and add a specific Cereja observation.

Guard: do not turn the feed into a news ticker.

### F. Build / Lab

Job: show how Cereja itself is being built when the process teaches something useful.

Guard: this cannot dominate the editorial property or turn it into a B2B engineering feed.

## 5. Carousel anatomy — modular, not templated

A strong carousel normally needs these **functions**, but not a fixed slide count or order:

1. **entry object** — what is this actually about?
2. **tension / curiosity gap** — why keep going?
3. **evidence / scene / example**
4. **development** — what changed or what is commonly missed?
5. **connection** — when Cereja has a real one
6. **useful implication / reference**
7. **exit** — question, next object, source, edition or action.

A 4-frame carousel can be complete. A 9-frame carousel is valid only when the material needs it.

### Hook rule

The cover should make the object legible before making it dramatic.

Prefer:
- a surprising concrete fact;
- a specific contradiction;
- a named object/person/event;
- a question Kell genuinely had;
- an image that creates the curiosity gap.

Avoid:
- "Você não está preparado para...";
- "Ninguém está falando sobre...";
- arbitrary numbered lists;
- vague transformation promises;
- engagement-bait questions.

## 6. Shareability without share-bait

Before approving a post, ask:

> Who would send this to whom, and why?

Valid reasons:
- "this reminded me of you";
- "you were talking about this";
- "this explains the thing we couldn't explain";
- "look at this weird/beautiful detail";
- "we should try/read/watch this";
- "this changes how I see the object".

If the only answer is "because the caption asks them to share", the post is not yet send-worthy.

## 7. Visual grammar

Shared Flame DNA:
- cherry / lime / ink;
- editorial typography roles;
- strong image direction;
- asymmetry when hierarchy benefits;
- provenance near factual/reference media;
- meaningful whitespace;
- one dominant visual idea per frame.

Instagram divergence:
- 4:5 feed is a composition canvas, not a shrunk webpage;
- text density may vary strongly between frames;
- not every frame needs heading + body + footer;
- imagery should carry narrative weight;
- a sequence may shift crop, scale and rhythm.

### Hard anti-template checks

Flag accumulation of:
- identical white card repeated on every frame;
- one big serif title + small sans body as the whole identity;
- pills everywhere;
- icon + title + paragraph grids;
- generic "premium" gradients;
- generic AI collage used across unrelated subjects;
- frame numbers used as the main design idea;
- repeated CTA slide with no editorial function.

## 8. Originality / third-party media

Cereja can discuss and reference external culture without becoming a repost account.

Every third-party asset requires:
- creator/source;
- rights/reuse status;
- transformation/editorial purpose;
- accessible description;
- disclosure when conceptual/generated.

When rights are unclear:
- use original typography/diagram;
- use public-domain/appropriately licensed material;
- link/reference instead of copying;
- create an original explanatory visual.

## 9. Reels

Use Reels when time, motion, sound or demonstration adds meaning.

Valid jobs:
- demonstrate an interface/tool;
- show a visual transformation;
- narrate a short connection;
- compare references;
- explain a mechanism with timed visual evidence;
- show a build/process.

Avoid:
- converting a carousel into auto-scrolling text;
- decorative B-roll over an essay;
- trend participation with no Cereja editorial reason.

No arbitrary "3-second hook" rule becomes canonical without account evidence.

## 10. Stories

Stories are the relationship layer.

Use for:
- edition launch/link;
- behind-the-scenes curation;
- polls that actually inform future coverage;
- a source or object that did not fit the feed;
- responses/questions;
- event/build context.

Avoid empty highlights, fake urgency and constant reposts with no added value.

## 11. Profile architecture

Bio should make clear what Cereja Flamejante is, why follow, who curates it and where to read.

Highlights should emerge from real Stories:
- Comece
- Edições
- Repertório
- Bastidores
- Kell

Do not create empty highlights for symmetry.

## 12. Experiment system

Evaluate **Cereja against Cereja**, not against viral creator benchmarks.

For every feed post record:
- format;
- content job;
- topic/object;
- reach;
- followers vs non-followers when available;
- watch time / retention when applicable;
- likes;
- sends/shares;
- saves;
- comments;
- profile visits;
- follows;
- outbound click when measurable;
- rights/provenance status;
- qualitative replies.

Derived rates when compatible:
- likes / reach;
- sends / reach;
- saves / reach;
- follows / profile visits or reach when the denominator is explicit.

Do not infer statistical significance from a handful of posts.

## 13. Outlier review

Every 4–6 feed posts, identify:
- best sends-per-reach;
- best saves-per-reach;
- best retention;
- best qualified conversation;
- worst post and likely reason.

Then ask what editorial object caused the result, what hook/form helped, what should be reused as a principle rather than copied as a template, what attracted the wrong audience and what looked generic despite acceptable metrics.

A high-reach post can still be strategically poor.

## 14. Quality gates

Before publication:

### Editorial
- specific object;
- accurate evidence;
- real connection;
- no invented Kell experience;
- channel-native completeness.

### Distinctiveness
- could this exact post belong to any marketing/AI/culture account after logo swap?
- does the composition carry a real Cereja decision?
- does the sequence have uneven, material-led rhythm?

### Rights
- all media approved;
- provenance recorded;
- AI conceptual art disclosed where relevant.

### Accessibility
- readable contrast;
- alt/caption package;
- text is not excessively small;
- information does not depend on color alone;
- motion has captions/no-motion meaning.

### Growth integrity
- hook matches delivery;
- no false scarcity;
- no clickbait gap that the post does not close;
- CTA matches the content job.

## 15. One-person operating model

Per newsletter cycle, default to:
- 1 launch Story sequence;
- 1 flagship feed piece chosen from the strongest discovery;
- 0–1 additional feed experiment only when the material justifies it;
- 0–1 Reel only when motion/audio genuinely adds value.

This is a ceiling, not a quota. Do not post weak material to satisfy cadence.
