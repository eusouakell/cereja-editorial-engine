# Cereja Flamejante Company Marketing Plan V2

Status: working company-level plan for Kell approval.  
Date: 2026-10-04.

This plan sits **above** [Distribuição e crescimento da Cereja Flamejante](distribution-plan.md), which remains the operating plan for the current editorial property.

## Marketing objective

Build a public, compounding system that:

1. makes Kell's expertise legible and associated with Context Engineering;
2. grows an owned audience without flattening Cereja Flamejante into B2B content;
3. turns Lab work into evidence and reusable IP;
4. generates speaking, collaboration, professional and commercial inbound;
5. creates validated paths into Academy, Studio and Products.

## Audience and progressive disclosure

### C-level / executives

Content jobs:
- interpret change;
- frame decision;
- expose risk/opportunity;
- connect AI/knowledge/work to business consequences;
- offer credible adoption paths.

### Builders / practitioners / deeply curious

Content jobs:
- show architecture;
- explain decisions;
- share tools and repositories;
- expose evals and failures;
- teach implementation.

### Organizers, media, partners and buyers

Do not rewrite every publication for them. Provide discoverable evidence and clear conversion surfaces so they can verify expertise and initiate contact.

## Content system

### Property: Cereja Flamejante

Keep its editorial freedom:
- culture;
- technology;
- science;
- work;
- unexpected connections;
- author-led curation.

### Personal authority series

Use LinkedIn first for recurring posts such as:
- **How I built this** — systems, agents, evals, workflows and product choices;
- **What failed and what changed** — postmortems with real decisions;
- **Context Engineering in practice** — applied examples, not vocabulary-only explainers;
- **Knowledge & Learning Systems** — how information becomes usable by people and machines;
- **Executive interpretation** — implications for work, leadership and organizations;
- **Field notes** — early signals, emerging practices and historical perspective.

These are not mandatory weekly columns. They are reusable content jobs.

## Automation boundary

Automate heavily before the external channel boundary:
- signal capture;
- research;
- packet assembly;
- derivative proposals;
- rights/accessibility checks;
- UTM generation;
- evals;
- scheduling preparation;
- performance aggregation.

Keep publication, consequential outreach, spend and brand/canonical decisions human-gated until evidence justifies a narrower autonomous scope.

## One-person sustainability

Every new recurring surface creates production, review and maintenance cost.

Before adding a channel/property, ask:
1. Does it have a distinct job?
2. Is there repeated demand?
3. Can existing material feed it without mechanical duplication?
4. Can Kell sustain the approval burden?
5. What will be stopped or reduced if this starts?

## Roadmap

### Phase 0 — strategy + proof

- approve Strategy V2;
- execute next newsletter cycle with current Engine;
- use the cycle to test evals and distribution;
- establish baseline;
- preserve Lab evidence.

### Phase 1 — instrument + make authority visible

- implement GA4/UTM architecture;
- begin personal build-in-public posts;
- assemble authority evidence inventory;
- define speaking topics/evidence;
- plan portfolio rebuild.

### Phase 2 — expose reusable value

- identify one Product candidate from real work;
- publish a useful article/tool/framework;
- add an appropriate interest/lead path;
- observe who responds and why.

### Phase 3 — validate one paid offer

Choose one:
- Studio engagement;
- workshop;
- learning experience;
- toolkit/product.

Do not launch a catalog.

### Phase 4 — decide second editorial property

Use evidence from personal LinkedIn and company/product interest to decide whether a separate technical/commercial newsletter is warranted.

### Phase 5 — amplify

Only then evaluate:
- paid media;
- broader community;
- recurring proprietary events;
- additional editorial distribution.
