# Editorial gates

**Thesis** — clear point of view, synthesis or question; new/revised/already published?

**Voice** — specific to Kell/Cereja or generic AI copy?

**Evidence** — material claims traceable with scope/date preserved?

**Originality** — interpretation beyond source paraphrase?

**Intellectual honesty** — fact, inference, hypothesis and opinion distinguishable?

**Audience value** — changes understanding, decision or action?

**Rights** — text, images, charts and third-party material usable this way?

Human approval is required for publication, new thesis, sensitive disclosure, science/health interpretation, client/partner reference and consulting positioning.
