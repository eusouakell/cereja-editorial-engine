# Voice calibration — proposed human review

Status: proposed procedure; no evaluation has been run. Reference: [provisional observations](https://github.com/eusouakell/cereja-knowledge-system/blob/main/editorial/voice-observations.md).

Historical samples help discovery. Only criteria explicitly approved by Kell become normative. Freeze their source commit and approval record before evaluating drafts.

## Review record

For each approved criterion record: criterion ID, source/version, draft passage, reviewer, decision (pass/revise/not applicable), reason and suggested revision. Do not equate repeated keywords, section names or metaphors with semantic voice fidelity.

Before publication check:
- Any first-person experience is supplied or explicitly approved by Kell.
- External claims have separate evidence review.
- Tone supports the intended reader and purpose.
- Departures from a pattern can be justified; optional patterns are not failures.
- Thesis approval and publication approval are recorded separately.

With no approved criteria, the voice gate stays pending. Other research work may continue. No aggregate score or claim that a model reproduces Kell's voice is justified by this checklist.
