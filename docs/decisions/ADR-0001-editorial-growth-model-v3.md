# ADR-0001 — Editorial growth model V3

**Status:** proposed — merge implies acceptance  
**Decision owner:** Kell  
**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Scope:** Cereja Flamejante marketing + editorial operation

## Context

Cereja Flamejante already has an approved multiformat editorial model: newsletter as the richest recurring property, Editorial Packet as canonical working object, native channel composition, human publication gates and learn-back.

The operating plan now needs to incorporate:

- Medium and DEV.to as long-form distribution surfaces with distinct jobs;
- YouTube as an audiovisual authority/proof surface;
- explicit company-level OKRs;
- an 8-week tactical/operational calendar;
- Narrative Craft & Oral Storytelling as a cross-format capability;
- stronger discoverability and effort measurement;
- durable cross-repository decision governance so Cereja does not diverge from Bússola/Agentic Factory work occurring in parallel.

## Decision

1. **Newsletter remains the editorial pillar.** It is not the source text from which other channels are mechanically shortened.
2. **Editorial Packet remains the canonical cycle object.**
3. **Medium, DEV.to and YouTube enter as pilots**, not permanent volume commitments.
4. **Narrative Craft & Oral Storytelling becomes a guide + gate**, not a new agent. Story is selected only when it improves the material.
5. **Discoverability is considered at packet and channel-selection time**, without allowing SEO to override editorial truth or voice.
6. **The pilot is governed by 3 OKRs** covering authority/proof, owned audience/depth and sustainable operation.
7. **The 2026-10-12 → 2026-12-07 calendar is the operating pilot**. Channel output remains conditional on job fit and effort.
8. **Human publication authority remains with Kell.** Routine specialist disagreement should use the shared Factory convergence model before becoming a human escalation.
9. **Cross-repository governance follows Agentic Factory ADR-0001 / PR #37**. Cereja owns editorial truth; the Factory owns reusable orchestration/governance.

## Consequences

### Keep

- quinzenal newsletter;
- Instagram visual editorial discovery;
- Stories research desk;
- LinkedIn self-contained authority posts;
- Substack Notes;
- GitHub proof/methods;
- human publication gate;
- learn-back.

### Pilot

- Medium;
- DEV.to;
- YouTube;
- Narrative Craft guide/gate;
- explicit effort budget and keep/adjust/stop review.

### Reject as default

- mandatory Reel;
- daily social volume;
- mechanical cross-posting;
- channel-specific agents before real evidence;
- automatic publication;
- using storytelling formulas on material that is better as reference, argument or explanation.
