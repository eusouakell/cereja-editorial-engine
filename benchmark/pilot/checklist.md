# Editorial review checklist

Distinguish sourced fact, interpretation, hypothesis and opinion. Preserve source dates, population, task and limits. Keep historical claims separate from reviewed evidence.

Check whether the question helps an editorial decision. Include a substantive counterargument and identify unresolved evidence. Do not substitute lexical novelty for originality or infer voice fidelity from repeated expressions.

Do not invent personal experience, credentials or author approval. Verify text and image rights before eventual publication. No image or long quotation is required in this brief.

Voice scoring, thesis approval and publication approval remain pending. This checklist supports preparation; it is not evidence of a completed human review.
