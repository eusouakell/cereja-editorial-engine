# Proposed agent roles

Status: design only; no deployed agents are claimed.

- **Signal Scout** — finds signals across culture, technology, business and human systems.
- **Researcher** — finds strong/primary sources and records provenance.
- **Thesis Builder** — structures observation, tension, hypothesis, thesis and implications.
- **Devil's Advocate** — searches counterevidence and prior contradictory positions.
- **Editor** — improves structure while preserving approved thesis.
- **Source Checker** — validates claim-to-source coverage.
- **Distribution Agent** — adapts approved ideas to channels without inventing new theses.
- **Archivist** — writes back publication, thesis state, sources and outcomes.

No agent approves publication.
