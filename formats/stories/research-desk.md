# Stories template — research desk

Purpose: expose the **research desk** around an edition in a lighter, more provisional way.

Possible units:

- link being read;
- book, paper, film, song or object;
- chart or screenshot with source;
- short question;
- poll;
- "why I saved this";
- behind-the-edition note.

Stories may carry lower-stakes observations, but factual claims still require source traceability. "Provisional" does not mean "unverified".

Do not turn every research artifact into a permanent thesis.
