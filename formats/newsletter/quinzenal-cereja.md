# Newsletter template — quinzenal Cereja

Status: **return template with archive-based composition guidance, pending Kell's review**.

Cadence: quinzenal for the first three returning editions; cadence can be reviewed after observed effort and editorial value.

## Required input

- one editorial packet;
- approved thesis or explicit open question;
- current evidence for material claims;
- counterpoint when the subject warrants it;
- voice guidance;
- rights status for third-party material.

## Grammar

```text
OPENING
→ CURATORIAL ENTRY / APPROVED QUESTION OR THESIS
→ DISCOVERIES + EVIDENCE + CONNECTIONS
→ OPTIONAL EXISTING EDITORIAL SECTIONS
→ CLOSING / OPEN QUESTION
```

Existing sections remain available as editorial organisms, not mandatory slots:

- Cherry Bomb
- ALT+TAB
- On Fire
- Hot Startups
- AiaiAI

A section is a delivery format, not a knowledge domain.

## Constraints

- Do not force every existing section into every issue.
- Do not manufacture a personal anecdote to imitate historical voice.
- Cultural references should create a real connection, not decoration.
- Distinguish sourced fact, interpretation, hypothesis and opinion.
- Publication always requires Kell's approval.

## Composition from the archive

Use the [edition briefing](brief-template.md) and [section briefings](editorias/README.md). The [voice guide in Núcleo](https://github.com/eusouakell/cereja-knowledge-system/blob/main/editorial/voice-and-tone.md) is the source for author guidance.

An edition can combine a main path, an independent detour and a current-affairs radar. Each return to a subject should add information or a new lens. Do not make every section an essay repeating the same question. Personal first-person claims require evidence from Kell.

The archive informs composition, not current factual truth. Publication and voice calibration still require human review.
