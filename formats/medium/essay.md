# Medium — durable essay

**Status:** pilot format contract

## Job

Turn an approved Editorial Packet into a durable, self-contained essay when the material benefits from synthesis, reflection and a longer conceptual life beyond the newsletter cycle.

Medium is not a newsletter mirror.

## Use when

- there is a durable argument;
- scene/experience + evidence + reflection improve the piece;
- the topic can stand alone months later;
- the essay adds interpretation rather than merely reposting the edition.

Do not use when the only reason is "we need a Medium post this month".

## Minimum inputs

- approved packet/thesis or approved open question;
- evidence/provenance;
- Narrative Craft decision: story route or explicit no-story route;
- discoverability notes;
- rights/quote constraints;
- intended CTA.

## Composition

Possible structure:

```text
CONCRETE ENTRY / QUESTION
→ CONTEXT
→ ARGUMENT / TENSION
→ EVIDENCE / COUNTERPOINT
→ INTERPRETATION
→ IMPLICATION
→ OPEN OR COMPLETE ENDING
```

Structure is adaptive, not mandatory.

## Discoverability

Record:
- primary durable question/entity;
- title that remains accurate out of feed context;
- canonical/original destination when applicable;
- relevant public proof or source links;
- whether the piece is evergreen, timely or mixed.

Avoid keyword stuffing and duplicate publication that creates unclear source authority.

## Gate

- self-contained;
- durable value;
- no newsletter-dependency;
- thesis/evidence scope preserved;
- Narrative Craft adds value rather than formula;
- source/rights constraints preserved;
- CTA matches audience state;
- human approval required.
